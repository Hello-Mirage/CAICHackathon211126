Water proofing work at toilet with protective plaster : i. Surface preparation and bore 
packing: Preparing the inside surface  of core and in-between PVC pipe by roughening 
using suitable file. Cleaning the surface by wire brushing followed water jet to remove 
any laitance or loose flaky particles. ii. Wall Waterproofing : a. Chipping & cleaning of 
concrete surface to remove dust, oil, grease, loose paint/coating. B.Groove cutting at 
all wall joint for making halloring work. C. Applying a coat of styrene-butadiene based 
polymer coating using Nitobond SBR to enhance adhesion between the packing 
material and other surfaces and packing the gap using nonshrink cement polymer-
based grout using Conbextra GP2 up to the top surface of the bore whilst the Nitobond 
SBR is in tacky state and then sealing with epoxy/polyester sealant. iii. Halloring work: 
in wall joints with Fosroc Hydraproof or Equivalent (Single component acrylic polymer 
for cement based waterproof composite coating membrane) modified mortar. Any 
cement is required will be supplied by client at free of cost in halloring activity. But 
subject to reconciliation. iv. Coating Work: 1st day, One coat (1 coat) Fosroc Brushbond 
RFX (High Performance elastomeric cementitious waterproof coating)painting on entire 
surface. v. Next Day: 2nd coat Fosroc Brushbond RFX (High Performance elastomeric 
cementitious waterproof coating) painting on entire surface. Each coating of Brushbond 
RFX will be 90 degree to each other. (Application will be done as per manufacturer’s 
specification). Sprinkle fine aggregate immediately after the application of second coat. 
Cure the Brushbond RFX membrane for minimum 3 days as per normal curing practice.
Consumption of Fosroc Brushbond RFX –(15Kg + 5.088 Kg Pack) 12 Sqm – 14 Sqm of 
each pack. vi. Protective Plaster: The waterproofing surface shall be protected with 
stucco plaster of 3-6 mm. vii. Afterwards curing will be done as per normal practice. # 
Stucco, Cement, Sand will be supplied by client but subjected to reconciliation basis:

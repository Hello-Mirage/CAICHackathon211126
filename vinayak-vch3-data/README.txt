VINAYAK GROUP  |  AI-ASSISTED PROCUREMENT  |  DATA PACK
Vibe Coders Hackathon Edition 3, 21 November 2026

These are real working documents from Vinayak Group. They are shared only for
today's build. Do not post them, share them outside your team, or keep them
after the hackathon. Ask Vinayak before showing any of this in a portfolio.

Read the problem statement PDF first. This pack is the raw material for it.

01_process
  Vinayak_procurement_SOP_12_steps.xlsx
  How Vinayak does procurement today, in 12 steps, from site BOQ to work order.

02_site_requirements
  Two real requirements as they arrived from site, by email.
  A_  Waterproofing, Amara Tower-2: the email and the quantity sheet the site sent.
  B_  Internal road work, Aquina: the email, the BOQ sheet and the road drawing.
  Use these as examples of what comes in. In real life a requirement can also
  arrive as a WhatsApp text or a voice note. Make your own samples for those.

03_tender
  BOQ_Specification_Waterproofing_Amara_Tower-2_rates_blank.pdf
  The document that goes out to vendors. The rate column is empty. Vendors fill it.
  Sample_RFQ_email_to_a_vendor.txt
  The email that carried it, with Vinayak's six standard terms.

04_item_codes
  ERP_item_codes_waterproofing.xlsx
  Item codes and descriptions for waterproofing work from Vinayak's ERP.
  Match a requirement to a code here. Some descriptions are near duplicates.

05_vendors
  Contractor_list_Aquina.xlsx
  Contractor names on one project. Names only. No category, contact or rating.

06_quotations
  Four real quotations received for the Amara waterproofing work.
  Four vendors, four layouts. One is a scan. Quantities and dates differ
  between them. Reading and normalising these is the hard part.

What is NOT in this pack, on purpose
  Vinayak's own comparative statement. Building that is your job.
  Vendor past performance. Vinayak does not record it anywhere today.
  Decide how you would capture and score it.

You do not have to build all six steps. Build the whole chain rough, or two or
three steps properly. A human makes the final call.
